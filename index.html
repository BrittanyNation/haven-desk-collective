How it works

Three honest steps. The account is the door — everything behind it belongs to the member who signed in.

Step 1

Create an account

Pick a plan — Day Tripper, Regular, or Resident. Your password is hashed on the server, never stored as text.

Step 2

Sign in, stay signed in

A session token keeps you signed in for 7 days on this device. Signing out deletes it — that token stops working immediately.

Step 3

Book your space

Members pick a date, a start time, and up to 4 hours. Members see their discount live, then get a booking reference they can cancel.

Questions, answered plainly

Do I need an account to look around?

No. Browsing, searching, and saving spaces is open to everyone. You only sign in to book — that is the whole point of the members door.

What if I forget my password?

This demo includes a password-change form on your dashboard while signed in. A real reset-by-email flow is the natural next step after this project, not a faked one.

Can someone else cancel my booking?

No. The server checks that the booking belongs to the signed-in account before it cancels — a different member's token gets a 403.

The Loft Letter

One short email a month: new spaces, member hours, and what is happening in the loft. No spam, unsubscribe any time.
